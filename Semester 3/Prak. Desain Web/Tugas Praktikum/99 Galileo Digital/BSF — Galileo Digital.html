Film Cleaners

Cinetech BSF HYDRA

Some features of the BSF HYDRA include full digital control via touchscreen, no-sprocket capstans, and soft nap Dacron buffers that can be adjusted to apply cleaning pressure to each side of the film independently.

BSF HYDRA In Action

Cinetech UK has over 40 years of experience operating, upgrading and repairing all types of film cleaning equipment, across every manufacturer and solvent type. Based on numerous client requests, and a pre-order for 3 units, Cinetech’s engineering team embarked on the design and manufacture of a revolutionary film cleaner with never seen before features. The Cinetech BSF HYDRA Film Cleaner utilizes Isopar® G (hydronaptha) film cleaning solvent, rated ‘excellent’ by Kodak for cleaning performance.

Product Features

  • Compact and elegant table top design

  • Soft touch capstan drive (no sprockets)

  • 8, 16, 35mm film gauges with optional 65/70mm as a special order

  • 100 ft per minute running speed

  • 2100 ft roll capacity

  • Full digital control, operated via a colour touchscreen. Full data logging, remote diagnostics and software updates if required

  • Very low power consumption. Single-phase power, multi region capable. 225W/240VA, 2.2A@110V, 1A@240V

  • Cleaning costs $0.10-$0.20 per 2000 ft reel depending on film dirt level

  • Non-immersion cleaning system utilizing soft nap Dacron buffers for solvent application (2) and film drying (4)

  • 12 PTR’s (particle transfer rollers) of our own design and manufacture

  • Solvent transfer roller speed adjustable via touch screen

  • Solvent applicator buffer roller speed adjustable via touch screen

  • Drying buffer speed adjustable via touch screen

  • Film tension adjustable via touchscreen

  • Film buffer film contact distance adjustable via touchscreen

  • All direct stepper drive, no drive belts
